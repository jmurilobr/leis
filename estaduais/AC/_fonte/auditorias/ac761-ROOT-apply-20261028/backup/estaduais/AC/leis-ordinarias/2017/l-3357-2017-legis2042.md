style="font-family:&quot;Arial&quot;,sans-serif;">II</span></strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Arial&quot;,sans-serif;">&nbsp;- para a Bolsa AAFIs formação avançada, o valor mensal será de R$ 800,00 (oitocentos reais).</span></span></p><p style="margin:0cm;font-size:16px;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;text-align:justify;"><span style="font-size: 12px;"><span style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></span></p><p style="margin:0cm;font-size:16px;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;text-align:justify;"><span style="font-size: ambientais e/ou valorização cultural;</span></span></p><p style="margin:0cm;font-size:16px;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;text-align:justify;"><span style="font-size: 12px;"><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Arial&quot;,sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></strong></span></p><p style="margin:0cm;font-size:16px;font-family:&quot;Times New extensão agroflorestal indígena e/ou de representação da categoria dos agentes agroflorestais indígenas;</span></span></p><p style="margin:0cm;font-size:16px;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif;text-align:justify;"><span style="font-size: 12px;"><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Arial&quot;,sans-serif;">IV</span></strong><span
