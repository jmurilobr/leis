style="margin-top:0cm;margin-right:0cm;margin-bottom:0cm;margin-left:0cm;line-height:normal;font-size:16px;text-align:justify;"><span style="font-size: 12px;"><span style="">&nbsp;</span></span></p><p -</span></strong><span style="">&nbsp;incentivar a geração de trabalho de renda nas cooperativas de reciclagem do Estado; e</span></span></p><p Verde.</span></span></p><p style="margin-top:0cm;margin-right:0cm;margin-bottom:0cm;margin-left:0cm;line-height:normal;font-size:16px;text-align:justify;"><span style="font-size: 12px;"><span
